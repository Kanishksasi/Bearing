# Arm raise validation sheet

This sheet checks the arm raise angle that Bearing reports against a protractor.
It is a personal accuracy check of the app. It does not record names or any health information.

## What you need

- A phone with Bearing installed, on a stable stand or tripod
- A large clear protractor (180 degrees)
- A second phone or camera for the protractor photos
- Tape to mark the floor
- A pencil and a printed copy of this sheet

## Procedure

1. Put the phone on a stable stand with the screen facing the volunteer. The phone should be at chest height and upright. Bearing will not let the trial start if the phone is tilted more than 5 degrees.
2. Mark the volunteer's standing spot on the floor with tape. Use the same spot and the same distance for every volunteer and every attempt.
3. Give each volunteer a number (1, 2, 3, and so on). Write only the number on the sheet. Do not write names.
4. Open the Arm raise routine. Wait for the alignment message to say it is ready, then start.
5. The volunteer raises one arm out to the side and holds it at the highest point for about 2 seconds, then lowers it. They keep the body upright. Leaning more than the app limit will not count.
6. At the highest point, the second camera takes one photo of the protractor. Place the center of the protractor on the shoulder joint. Line the flat edge up with the torso, pointing down toward the hip. Read the angle where the upper arm crosses the scale. Keep faces out of the photo.
7. Write the protractor angle in the sheet for that attempt.
8. Repeat for three attempts on one side, then three attempts on the other side.
9. When the routine ends, read the result screen. The app shows the best and the average for each side. It does not show the angle for each single attempt, so the app column says "not shown" for attempts one to three. Copy the app best and average into the Best and Average rows.
10. Fill in the Best row of the protractor column with the largest of the three protractor angles. Fill in the Average row with their mean.
11. Work out the difference in each row that has both numbers: app angle minus protractor angle. A positive number means the app read higher.
12. Delete the photos after the angles are written down.

## Reading the summary

The mean difference shows whether the app tends to read higher or lower than the protractor. The spread shows how much the differences vary from row to row. A small mean with a small spread means the app and the protractor agree closely. These numbers describe this test setup only.
